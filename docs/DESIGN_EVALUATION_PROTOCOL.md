# Whole-city-first design acceptance protocol

## Mandatory scene families
- Five or more matched distant viewpoints: see ring, tiers, skyline, HERO4 and secondary districts.
- Street level: demonstrate scale, approach, entrance and how people cross vertical tiers.
- Aerial: confirm open voids versus high-density blocks, no box-like repeated patterns.
- Major interior: align atrium, circulation, skybridges, exterior openings and destinations.
- Day/night where palette or illumination decisions are involved.

## Deliberately NOT an aesthetic score
Visible roof pixel totals, sparse ray intersections, render diff percentage, proxy face contacts and QA count. These are diagnostic indicators only.

## Fail-closed safety, flexible aesthetics
Protect frozen geometry and reserved voids exactly. Do not relax LOCKED objects for extra beauty.
Compare all citywide proposals against M101 as current baseline, but DO NOT veto improvements merely because any individual visual pixel count shrinks.
Selection advances only if the urban form clearly improves; if all variants are weak, report HOLD rather than pretend completion.

## Change size
Each major batch must address city-scale composition or full-district outside/interior/circulation. Avoid endless 0.5 m roof adjustments.
