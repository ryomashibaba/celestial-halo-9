# CELESTIAL HALO-9 — PROJECT CONSTITUTION
Date: 2026-10-12. Status: USER-APPROVED LOCKED STRATEGY.

## Unchanging highest priorities
1. NO DIRECTION DRIFT — the citywide creative vision takes precedence over numerical proxies, local roof optimization and process bureaucracy.
2. MAXIMUM EFFICIENT PROGRESS — large cohesive outputs and actual completed districts, not maximum QA counts or M-number increments.

## Eight locked creative decisions
1. A RING MEGASTRUCTURE, monumental building clusters and multiple vertical tiers must form one composition.
2. BRIGHT MONUMENTAL architecture contrasted against DARK INDUSTRIAL machinery.
3. Deliberate contrast of high urban density and vast open spatial voids.
4. Protected and frozen shapes never silently change; the UNAPPROVED design relationships among the 16 sites may be fundamentally redesigned.
5. All 16 buildings have principal interiors, and HERO4 receive detailed interiors.
6. One safe pilot region may be constructed ONLY after a separate user approval of its exact area, change set and rollback; design may continue in parallel.
7. A distinct fusion of FUTURE SF, MYSTICISM and INDUSTRIAL POWER.
8. Distant citywide grandeur AND rewarding pedestrian/building exploration have equal standing.

## Authorized development scope
Offline architecture, scripts, tests, work-branch pushes and draft PR creation are authorized. Formal new design baselines, project governance changes, Freeze changes, and actual Minecraft construction require separate user consent. Never treat a CI PASS as authorization to deploy.

The current comparison baseline is M101 TERRACED DISTRICT RIM, not an aesthetic veto. M105-M107 are NON-CONSTRUCTION research proposals. Preserve Freeze408; 959 existing public meshes; M81 piers; 24 historic porticos; 88 reserved voids; SB09 upper gate/wall/opening; SB13 3x4 entry; H70-H80 guides; accepted road masks. ## Review rule
Aesthetic decisions require whole-city scenes, street-level views, coherent interiors and exploration. Pass counts, roof-visible pixels, model contact proxies or speculative support beams never establish beauty, structural capacity or construction readiness. Every changed approved baseline must include rationale, visual comparison, safety audit and explicit user approval.

This constitution takes precedence over all generated work plans but never bypasses immutable protection boundaries.
