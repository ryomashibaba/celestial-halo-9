## Crucial lesson
Changing giant structures from “floating pads on piers” to “continuous stacked urban masses” did not solve urban form quality. Neither visual method develops an authentic city-architectural grammar. Repetition, strong blank masses and nominal programs still dominate. Further detailing of either D2 shape would repeat S23–S26 failures.
