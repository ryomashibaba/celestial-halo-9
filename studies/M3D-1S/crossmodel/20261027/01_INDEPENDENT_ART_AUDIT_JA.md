## Independent conclusion
**C55-C: preserve as an unusually promising _spatial principle_, not as an approved outer silhouette.** Its upside-down lived-world geometry successfully breaks away from historical cathedral typology. Its downside is the distant knotted black sculpture effect, generic box city and weak sacred quality.

**R32: stop this specific repeated-urban-bank build**, as its own image review already concluded. Do not preserve the rejected R32 macro proportions as mandatory for later GPT/Work.

The strongest research question is not “which model won?” but: **Can the large-scale silhouette be physically generated by a convincing ultrafuturistic inhabited city, so its interior civilization and exterior artwork are inseparable?**
